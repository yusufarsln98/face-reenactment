Assignment: Exploring DeepFakes

Your task is to implement a DeepFake machine learning model — or any model that achieves a comparable face reenactment or identity-mapping effect.
The assignment is designed to explore how far the brightest and most ambitious minds can go when working as a team in a short period of time.
Rules & Freedom

You may use any tools, libraries, pretrained models, or AI assistants you like.
However, you must fully understand what you are doing — and be able to explain the core logic, not just run existing code.
You are strongly encouraged to:
explore different architectures (autoencoder, GAN, diffusion, keypoint-based, etc.),
document every choice and turning point,
include failed experiments and discuss why they failed,
and reflect on what each experiment taught you.
Output Requirements

Generate short, light-hearted demonstration videos (keep them ethical, safe, and consent-based — don’t get us into trouble!).
Accompany your work with clear documentation and a presentation.
After the Assignment, You Should Be Able To

Explain how your model works (conceptually).
Describe its architecture and data flow.
Discuss how training and evaluation are performed.
(Hint: smart students will recognize how this ties into the final exam topics.)
Have a working version on your own computer that can provide endless joy to your friends and family.
Group Formation

Group 1: Students with student ID% 3 == 0
Group 2: Students with student ID% 3 == 1
Group 3: Students with student ID% 3 == 2

Remember — this is a perfect opportunity to bond with your classmates. Teamwork will be part of the assessment.
Deliverables

Class Presentation (≈30 minutes)
Demonstrate your results.
Explain your approach and lessons learned.
Discuss limitations and ethical implications.
Written Report
Describe what you did, how you did it, and what you learned.
Use your own words (no AI assistance in the report text, I can write better prompts than you, and have no interest in reading GPT bullshit filler).
Include evidence of at least two team meetings (Online or Face to Face) — screenshots, photos, or kebab receipts accepted.
You may exclude inactive members from authorship.
Each student has to submit the report (even if it is the same report).
Optional (but impressive)

Include a comparison between methods (e.g., Autoencoder vs GAN vs Diffusion).
Add a DeepFake detection experiment or artifact analysis.
Discuss ethical challenges and countermeasures.

Do not forget to have fun!
